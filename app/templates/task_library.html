Custom Task

Define your own task by providing a prompt. Scored or unscored. Available in both Pure AI and HITL builders.

In either builder, select Custom Task… from the task dropdown (Step 1). Enter your instructions in the prompt field that appears. If your task has a ground-truth answer, mark it as scored — the platform will capture numeric output and compute agreement statistics. If outcomes are qualitative, mark it unscored to disable scoring.
